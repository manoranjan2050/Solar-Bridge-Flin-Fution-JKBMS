About BMS Control

The JK BMS is connected in passive read-only mode (RS485 broadcast). Charge/discharge behaviour is controlled through the Inverter settings above — setting the bulk/float/cutoff voltages here tells the inverter how to treat the battery. Direct BMS parameter writing (cell balance thresholds, protection limits) requires the JK active command protocol and is not currently implemented.

What is Tailscale? How do I see my solar from anywhere? (click to open guide)

Tailscale creates a free, private, encrypted network between your own devices — like a secret tunnel between your phone and this Pi. Nothing is opened to the public internet, there is no port-forwarding, and only devices signed into YOUR account can connect.

Setup on your phone / laptop (one time):

  1. Install the Tailscale app — Android · iPhone · Windows/Mac
  2. Sign in with the same account you used to authorise this Pi (Google / Microsoft / GitHub)
  3. Switch the Tailscale toggle ON in the app
  4. Open the Remote URL shown above in your browser — your dashboard, from anywhere 🌍

Good to know:

  • Works on mobile data, office WiFi, anywhere — no router setup needed
  • The "LAN IP" works only at home; the "Remote URL" works everywhere
  • Manage your devices at login.tailscale.com
  • Your dashboard password still protects the settings — keep it on
  • "Turn Off" only pauses remote access; "Turn On" resumes it instantly

How it works

Rules run on the bridge every poll cycle. Threshold rules fire when a sensor crosses a value (e.g. SOC < 30 → charger to Solar+Grid). Time rules fire once a day at a set time. Saving rules restarts the bridge so they take effect.
